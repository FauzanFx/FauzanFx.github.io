## Project Overview
Voyager is a semi-autonomous riverboat project dedicated to cleaning up riverways by collecting floating trash. The vessel is capable of being maneuvered via a remote control application as well as operating autonomously using computer vision. This initiative gained significant traction and was officially presented to the Mayor of Yogyakarta.

## Responsibilities & Technical Contributions
As part of the engineering team, I focused on the vessel's propulsion control, electrical integrity, and system integration:
- **Motor Control Programming**: Programmed Arduino microcontrollers to generate precise PWM signals, controlling two Electronic Speed Controllers (ESCs) to ensure accurate and responsive maneuvering from the dual thrusters.
- **Electrical & Wiring Architecture**: Executed the complete electrical wiring linking the microcontrollers to the ESCs and safely distributed the direct power supply to both thrusters.
- **System Integration**: Conducted rigorous system checks to bridge the communication between the Arduino and the Raspberry Pi, which serves as the vessel's main processing core for vision and high-level autonomous logic.
- **Hardware Reliability & Troubleshooting**: Performed structural troubleshooting on the vessel's physical body, specifically identifying and sealing hull leaks to safeguard the internal electronics from water damage.
